I recently passed my PhD viva at Queen Mary University of London (formal award pending). My thesis, 'Bridging Graph and Dense Representations for Coronary Vessel Segmentation and Registration' was supervised by [Dr. Qianni Zhang](https://scholar.google.co.uk/citations?user=XR6C9BoAAAAJ&hl=en), [Prof. Greg Slabaugh](http://www.eecs.qmul.ac.uk/~gslabaugh/) and [Prof. Christos Bourantas](https://scholar.google.co.uk/citations?user=rQn8RpgAAAAJ&hl=en)

I am currently a postdoctoral fellow in the [Visual Computing](https://orbit.dtu.dk/en/organisations/visual-computing) group at DTU Compute Copenhagen, under the supervision of [Prof. Rasmus Paulsen](https://www.imm.dtu.dk/~rapa/). 

My current research focus is the segmentation, registration and analysis of coronary vessels and plaque using graph and geometric representations. More broadly I am interested in:
1. Graph neural networks
2. Combining geometric and image representations
3. Multi-modality learning 


Outside of the lab, I enjoy long-distance running, and all things hilly / green.
